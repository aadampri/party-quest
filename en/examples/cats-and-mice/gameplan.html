Game Flow

  • The winner chooses who receives the next puzzle piece.
  • The recipient sticks their cheese symbol on the piece.
  • The last piece goes to the last child without a piece.
  • At the end, assemble all pieces and hunt for the treasure together.

Game 0 - Build a Cat Disguise

"Wait - Monsieur Moustache's traps only catch MICE! If we look like cats, they will not trigger. Quick - build your cat disguise, then prove you can move like a cat by carrying the cheese ball without dropping it!"
  • Craft: build a ping-pong racket from a cup and cardboard, decorated as a cat face.
  • Game: balance a ping-pong ball on the racket, go around a post, and return.
  • Dropped ball = restart or time penalty.
  • Fastest time wins.
PlayerTimePlace

Winner chooses the recipient for puzzle piece #1.

Game 1 - Backward Escape

"We are at the entrance! But look - a mirror alarm. It only spots mice who walk FORWARD. If we go backward, we are invisible. Who is the fastest backward mouse?"
  • Walk backward from start, around a post or tree, and back to start.
  • Fastest time wins.
PlayerTimePlace

Winner chooses the recipient for puzzle piece #2.

Game 2 - The Cat Code

"We are inside! But the next door has a number lock. The code changes every time. Guess the secret number, then we roll the dice to see who cracked the code!"
  • Each player guesses a sum from 3 to 18.
  • Roll 3 dice.
  • Closest guess wins; ties can roll again.
  • Play several rounds if needed.

Dice result: _______ - winner chooses the recipient for puzzle piece #3.

Game 3 - Fire Trap

"Oh no - the cat set a FIRE TRAP in the kitchen! We must put out the flames before the map pieces burn. Grab the water pistols - the best firefighter wins!"
  • Hang one sponge per player on a clothesline at chest height.
  • Weigh each sponge before and after on a kitchen scale.
  • Use identical water pistols, filled equally.
  • Fixed distance, 15 seconds.
  • Heaviest sponge after shooting wins.

Biggest difference wins and chooses the recipient for puzzle piece #4.

Game 4 - Guard Dog Snack

"We made it into the garden - but the guard dog blocks the way! He lets us pass only if we bring his favorite snack at exactly the right weight. Mix your ingredients carefully!"
  • Use a mud kitchen or outdoor mixing area plus a kitchen scale.
  • Announce a target weight, for example 500 g.
  • Each player scoops 3 ingredients: sand, stones, leaves, and so on.
  • Weigh the bowl; closest to target wins.
  • No adjusting after placing the bowl on the scale.

Game 5 - Rooftop Escape

"ALARM! Monsieur Moustache is coming home early! We must escape over the rooftops NOW. Over the bars, down the slide, around the tower - RUN! The fastest mouse escapes with the final piece!"
  • Course: down the slide, to the climbing bars, across or under, touch the post, climb up, and high-five the top marker.
  • One player at a time against the clock.
  • Bypass for hard climbing bars is allowed with a small time penalty.
PlayerTimePlace

Winner chooses the recipient for puzzle piece #6. The last piece goes to the child without a piece.

Finale - Puzzle and Treasure Hunt

"We did it! We have ALL the pieces. Quick - assemble the map before the cat comes back!"

Children assemble the puzzle.

"There! That is where the cheese is hidden. Let's go!"

Run together to the place. Each child finds the treasure marked with their cheese symbol.

E1 - Cheese Shelf Trap

"A trap! The cat rigged the cheese shelf so it collapses. Cheese balls are falling - catch them with your disguise before they touch the floor and trigger the alarm!"
  • Throw ping-pong balls and catch them with the cat racket.
  • Most catches in 10 tries wins.

E2 - Sticky Trap

"The cat spilled glue all over the hall! Your feet are stuck together. Jump as far as you can to cross the sticky zone!"
  • Standing long jump with both feet together.
  • Longest landing wins.
